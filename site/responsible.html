Responsible use

What "uncensored" really means

No model is uncensored in the absolute sense. Every model reflects the data and choices of whoever trained it. The honest question is: whose choices, and who answers for the results?

Tell

Closed-weight vendors make that choice for you and answer for it. With open weights, you make it and you carry the legal, ethical and reputational responsibility for what the model produces. Over-refusal is a real cost: it blocks researchers, writers, clinicians and lawyers. Under-refusal is a real cost too: it can produce material that harms people. The skill is calibration.

Do

Before training, complete this checklist:

  • Written policy with named owner (SOP Step 1)
  • Licence checked for the base model and the use case
  • Dataset reviewed by a human, with kept-refusal examples included
  • Evaluation covering both over-refusal and under-refusal (SOP Step 6)
  • Access limited to the intended audience, with logging (SOP Step 8)
  • Local law and your employer's or customers' rules reviewed
Apply

If you cannot say who is allowed to use the adapted model and what it must never do, you are not ready to deploy it. Start with a prompt change, move to LoRA only when the evidence says you need it, and keep the guards.
